LiveView Duo

Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

LiveView Duo turns a second iPhone into a camera monitor. Everything it does happens between two devices you own.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising identifier, no crash reporting service, and no server of ours anywhere in the product.

Your video and photos

Video and audio travel directly between your two iPhones over your local network — or, when no network is available, over a direct device-to-device connection. That connection is encrypted and authenticated with a pairing code that you choose and that only your devices know.

Nothing is uploaded. There is no cloud component to upload to.

Recordings are saved to your own photo library, on the iPhone doing the recording. The app never reads your photo library; it can only add to it.

Permissions

CameraTo show what you are framing and send it to your other iPhone.
MicrophoneTo record sound alongside your video.
PhotosAdd-only access, to save what you record. The app cannot see existing photos.
Local networkTo find your other iPhone and pair with it.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
